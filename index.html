01 / Our story

IT STARTED WITH THE MUSIC.

Dance Culture began in Kent in 2014. Not around hype. Not around algorithms. Around the feeling of the right record, in the right room, with the right people.

What began predominantly in drum & bass grew into underground parties bringing hundreds of people together. Before COVID, we regularly drew crowds of 400–800.

But the thing that made Dance Culture special was never just the numbers. It was the crowd. Someone came, brought a friend, and that friend brought more.

That is still the point. Create the room. Find the music. Let the culture grow.
